Change your car's LibertyCityPlates plate in-game, and keep it on your saved cars.

**Install:** unzip, copy `PlateChanger.asi` and `PlateChanger.ini` next to `LibertyCityPlates.asi` in your game folder. Works on GTA IV Complete Edition 1.2.0.59 with FusionFix (and should on 1.0.x/1.1.x).

**Use:** press **F11** in a car or after leaving one, type the plate (it shows at the bottom of the screen), **Page Up/Down** for the plate picture, **Home/End** for the font, **Enter** when done.

### What's in this version
- Type the plate in-game. No popup window, so no black screen with FusionFix.
- Change the plate picture and font while editing; each picture gets its preset text size, position and colour from your `vehicles.ide`.
- The pictures and fonts you can step through are read from your own `vehicles.ide`, so added plates are picked up automatically and stock setups only see the stock ones.
- Plates you set stay on your saved cars: they're remembered in `PlateChanger.plates` and put back when the game re-creates the car where it was parked.

Needs [LibertyCityPlates](https://github.com/ImpossibleEchoes/LibertyCityPlates) and an ASI loader (FusionFix has one). See the README for settings and troubleshooting.
